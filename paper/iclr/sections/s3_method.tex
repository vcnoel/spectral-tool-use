\section{Reading the model at three levels}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Task and labels.}
\label{sec:method:task}
A model receives a user request and tool schemas in its own chat format and generates greedily. We compare
the call against the benchmark's ground truth after normalising both to a canonical name and argument
dictionary, and record a \emph{failure mode} rather than a bit: \texttt{valid}, \texttt{wrong\_name},
\texttt{wrong\_arg\_values}, \texttt{missing\_args}, \texttt{missing\_calls}, \texttt{no\_call},
\texttt{unparseable\_call}, and, where no tool applies, \texttt{valid\_nocall} and \texttt{over\_trigger}. The
two format failures, \texttt{no\_call} and \texttt{unparseable\_call}, are detectable from length alone, so every
result is reported on the \emph{semantic} subset that excludes them, where a detector must separate a correct
call from a well-formed wrong one; where the benchmark mixes in the no-applicable-tool category we further
restrict to the \emph{call-expected} items. Calls cut off by our generation budget are excluded.

\paragraph{Access tiers.}
\label{sec:method:tiers}
Token log-probabilities are returned by every serving API; residual-stream states are a forward hook away in
a stack that runs the model's own code but are not returned by hosted APIs; attention probabilities are the
most awkward, since fused kernels never materialise them (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}). We group detectors by
what they consume, \emph{logits}, \emph{attention} (functions of the attention weights alone) and
\emph{residual} (functions of the block outputs), and measure what each costs (Appendix~\ref{app:cost}).
Keeping the tiers apart requires care: a family of published ``attention'' diagnostics projects residual states
onto the attention eigenbasis \citep{noel2025gsp}, and in prior work built on that library four of five
nominally spectral features were of this hybrid kind. Appendix~\ref{app:provenance} tags every feature by its
tensors.

\paragraph{Attention-only detectors.}
\label{sec:method:attn}
All are computed on the subgraph induced on the generated call, using Eq.~\ref{eq:adj} and the normalised
Laplacian. \emph{Per-head spectral profile (ours)}: for each layer $\ell$ and head $h$, one eigendecomposition
of $\mathcal{L}_{\ell h}$ yields five eigenvalue-only quantities, the Fiedler value $\lamtwo$, the ratio
$\lamtwo/\lammax$, the normalised spectral entropy of the eigenvalues, the eigenvalue mass in the upper half of
the spectrum, and the spectral radius $\lammax$; the feature vector is the $L\times H\times 5$ array and five
cost what one costs. \emph{Cross-layer dynamics (ours)}: per-head differences of a chosen quantity between
consecutive layers. \emph{Head-averaged summaries}: the per-layer values, trajectory statistics and
layer-to-layer derivatives the prior literature uses, retained as ablations. \emph{LapEigvals}
\citep{binkowski2025lapeigvals}: the published attention-only state of the art from the authors' code, per
layer and head the position-normalised in-degree minus self-loop, sorted, top-$k$ kept with $k$ chosen on
validation from $\{5,10,25,50,100\}$, and a balanced logistic regression. \emph{Lookback Lens}
\citep{chuang2024lookback}: per-head attention shares on prompt against generation.

\paragraph{Residual-stream detectors.}
\label{sec:method:resid}
\emph{Token-role probe} \citep{healy2026internal}: at eight depths spaced over the model's layers, the residual
state at the function-name token, the mean over argument-value tokens and the state at the closing delimiter
are concatenated and a classifier is trained on them; we evaluate the original one-hidden-layer network and a
regularised linear probe, with positions located inside the generated span only. \emph{Token-level probe}
\citep{obeso2025realtime}, \emph{Gram spectrum} \citep{chen2024inside} and \emph{cross-layer dynamics}
\citep{icrprobe2025} complete the tier.

\paragraph{Protocol.}
\label{sec:method:protocol}
(i) Examples are grouped by ground-truth tool and split at the group level in a five-fold cross-fit, so every
score is on tools absent from training; with prompt-level splits a classifier on the tool identity alone
reaches \aucToolConfoundLlamaOneBGlaiveAll{} AUC on one run. (ii) Each example is scored once by a model that
never saw its tool, one AUC is pooled over the dataset, and the procedure is repeated over five split seeds.
(iii) Every hyperparameter, the direction of every untrained score and the $k$ of LapEigvals are chosen on
training and validation folds only. (iv) Three deliberately uninformative classifiers, lengths with a
truncation flag, generation length alone, and the tool identity, are trained under the same protocol. (v)
Every difference between detectors carries a paired, class-stratified bootstrap interval over items, pooled
across seeds, Holm-corrected inside a contrast family fixed before any result was read
(Appendix~\ref{app:stats}). (vi) A run whose minority class is below \minClassPerSubset{} carries a dagger
and enters no aggregate. (vii) Labels are recomputed from the stored generations at evaluation time.
