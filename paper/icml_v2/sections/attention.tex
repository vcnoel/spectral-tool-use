\paragraph{Head averaging.}
Whether an attention readout works depends on whether heads are averaged before anything is computed. The head-averaged spectral profile, the usual read of a layer in this literature and the one \wscitet{} found strong, sits within \havgFloorMinInternals{} to \havgFloorMaxInternals{} of the length floor on the probe-side runs. On the stored per-head features of \ladNRuns{} runs, keeping heads apart scores \ladPerHead{} against \ladMetricAvg{} for the same metrics averaged over heads, a gain that is positive on \ladGainPerHeadPos{} of \ladNRuns{} runs. Permuting the head axis independently per item, which keeps every value and destroys only which head produced it, costs \ladGainVsShuffle{}, and padding the averaged features with noise to the per-head width does not recover it (\cref{app:ladder}).

The construction behind this is elementary, and \cref{app:proofs} states it for causal attention. Take a previous-token head and a sink head, both causal and row-stochastic. Replacing both by their average gives a second configuration with the same head-averaged graph. Any statistic of the averaged graph returns the same value on both, while the spread of per-head algebraic connectivity is zero in one and tends to one half in the other as the sequence grows (\cref{prop:annihilation}). For the combinatorial Laplacian the averaged graph can only overstate the mean connectivity over heads (\cref{prop:jensen}). The normalised Laplacian the detectors use has no such bound: in the same construction at $T=\ccTlarge{}$ the averaged graph reads \ccNormAvgLarge{} against a per-head mean of \ccNormMeanLarge{}, and on \jensenLayers{} measured layers the bound fails on \jensenNormViolations{}.

\paragraph{Cost.}
Reading confidence costs nothing beyond generation. Every internal judge needs a teacher-forced pass over prompt and call, \latPass{}\,ms against \latGen{}\,ms of generation (\cref{fig:attention}b). On top of the pass the token-role features cost \latTokenRole{}\,ms and LapEigvals \latLapEig{}\,ms. The per-head spectra cost \latPerHead{}\,ms at the median and \latPerHeadNinety{}\,ms at the 90th percentile, more than generation when the call is long. The output-only judge needs no pass of the deployed model, and the reader model needs one pass of \readerName{} over the request and the call.
