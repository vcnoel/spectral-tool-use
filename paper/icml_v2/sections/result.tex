\cref{tab:main} and \cref{fig:types} hold the paper's measurement. On every failure, the \nRunsInternals{} powered Llama-3.2 and Gemma-3 runs have an internal advantage of \gapInternalsMin{} to \gapInternalsMax{}, with an interval excluding zero on \nInternalsCiExcludesZero{} of them. The \nRunsConfidence{} powered Qwen3, Qwen3.5 and MiniCPM5 runs read \gapConfidenceMin{} to \gapConfidenceMax{}, with an interval below zero on \nConfidenceCiBelowZero{}. Runs that share a checkpoint are not independent, so the unit of any test is the checkpoint. With \nCkptInternals{} against \nCkptConfidence{} checkpoints the smallest attainable two-sided rank-test value is $p=\ckptExactFloor{}$, so the separation is a description of these \nCkpt{} checkpoints.

\paragraph{Wrong argument values.}
The pooled comparison mixes kinds of error whose sign differs, so \cref{fig:types}b scores each against valid calls alone. On wrong argument values the probe leads confidence on \nWavInternalsPos{} of the \nInternalsRuns{} probe-side runs, by \wavInternalsMin{} to \wavInternalsMax{}, with an interval excluding zero on \nWavInternalsCiExcl{}. The exception is Llama-3.2-1B on the live categories at \WavGapLlamaOneBLive{}. Confidence leads the probe on all \nWavConfidenceRuns{} confidence-side runs, native and forced into JSON, where the advantage is \wavConfidenceMin{} to \wavConfidenceMax{}. On these models the mean log-probability already ranks most wrong values below right ones. Over every failure, an equal-weight fusion of probe and confidence gains \fusConfMinConfidence{} to \fusConfMaxConfidence{} over confidence alone on the confidence side, with no interval excluding zero, against \fusConfMinInternals{} to \fusConfMaxInternals{} on the probe side.

\paragraph{Dropped parallel calls.}
On all \nMcRuns{} runs with at least twenty dropped calls the probe leads confidence, by \mcGapMin{} to \mcGapMax{} (\cref{fig:types}c). Valid calls outside the parallel categories cannot drop a call, so part of that lead is a category contrast. Within the parallel categories the lead holds on MiniCPM5-2B and Qwen3.5-0.8B under forced JSON, \PwGapMiniCpmBfclJson{} [\PwGapLoMiniCpmBfclJson{}, \PwGapHiMiniCpmBfclJson{}] and \PwGapQwenThreeFiveBfclJson{} [\PwGapLoQwenThreeFiveBfclJson{}, \PwGapHiQwenThreeFiveBfclJson{}], and vanishes on Llama-3.2-3B at \PwGapLlamaThreeBBfcl{} [\PwGapLoLlamaThreeBBfcl{}, \PwGapHiLlamaThreeBBfcl{}]. Gemma-3 makes \pwNegGemmaBfcl{} valid parallel calls and cannot be tested there.

\paragraph{Output format.}
Qwen3 writes JSON like Llama-3.2 and Gemma-3 and sits on the confidence side, so the native format does not decide the side. Registered prediction R3 said that, forced to write JSON, the confidence-side families keep a pooled advantage at or below zero, and that it fails above \rThreeThreshold{}. It failed. MiniCPM5-2B moved to \rThreeJsonMiniCpm{} [\rThreeJsonLoMiniCpm{}, \rThreeJsonHiMiniCpm{}] and Qwen3.5-0.8B to \rThreeJsonQwenThreeFive{} [\rThreeJsonLoQwenThreeFive{}, \rThreeJsonHiQwenThreeFive{}]. The shift is carried by dropped parallel calls, which make up \rThreeMcJsonMiniCpm{} of \rThreePosJsonMiniCpm{} failures and \rThreeMcJsonQwenThreeFive{} of \rThreePosJsonQwenThreeFive{} under forced JSON. On wrong argument values both models stay on the confidence side (\cref{tab:main}, last rows). The forced format changed which errors the models make and left their confidence on value errors where it was.

\paragraph{Size and release date.}
The probe side holds checkpoints of one and three billion parameters and the confidence side checkpoints of 0.8 to two. Within Llama-3.2 the probe does not improve from 1B to 3B, \aucProbeLlamaOneBBfcl{} at both sizes on BFCL and \aucProbeLlamaOneBGlaive{} against \aucProbeLlamaThreeBGlaive{} on Glaive, and the side does not change. Two properties of the model set do separate the sides on these six checkpoints: release date, with a cutoff between March and April 2025, and a reasoning mode in the chat template. \cref{sec:discussion} returns to both.

\paragraph{Calls with a history and replication.}
With earlier ground-truth calls in the prompt, on the multi-turn categories of BFCL-v4, MiniCPM5-2B reads \mtAucProbe{} for the probe and \mtAucConf{} for confidence, an advantage of \mtGapAll{} [\mtGapLoAll{}, \mtGapHiAll{}] opposite in sign to its single-turn runs, with tools and conversations linked into \mtGroupsAll{} groups. Its single-turn side is not reproduced with a history (\cref{app:multiturn}). Llama-3.2-1B on BFCL, extracted twice before and after the extraction repairs, reads \replGapStale{} and \replGapClean{}. Differences smaller than this drift of \replGapDelta{} are not interpreted.
